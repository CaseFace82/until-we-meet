UNTIL WE MEET — ART FOLDER

Create a folder named:

art

in the root of the GitHub repository.

WEEKLY HEADER FILES
-------------------
The app is prepared for one watercolor header for each gestational week:

header-week-10.webp
header-week-11.webp
header-week-12.webp
...
header-week-41.webp

That is 32 weekly header files total.

Recommended export:
- WEBP
- approximately 1200 × 750 px
- landscape orientation
- keep the LEFT side relatively quiet because the greeting/countdown text sits there
- main scenic/Oliver details work best toward the center/right
- no text baked into the artwork

The visual progression should feel like San Diego:
Weeks 10–13: late September / early October, warm coastal early fall
Weeks 14–17: late October, subtle autumn/Halloween touches
Weeks 18–21: November, golden coastal autumn / Thanksgiving warmth
Weeks 22–23: December, greener hills after rain / subtle holiday details
Weeks 24–28: late December and January, crisp San Diego winter
Weeks 29–31: February, early flowers / Valentine's touch around Week 30
Weeks 32–36: late February and March, green hills and wildflowers
Weeks 37–39: late March / April, bright spring coast
Weeks 40–41: late April / early May, special final spring scenes

OLIVER FILES
------------
The app is prepared for 12 transparent-background Oliver illustrations:

oliver-01.webp
oliver-02.webp
...
oliver-12.webp

Suggested poses:
01 happy sitting
02 head tilt
03 lying down / paws forward
04 looking to the side
05 favorite toy
06 beach pose
07 little hiking/adventure pose
08 cozy/sleepy
09 subtle holiday bandana
10 spring flowers
11 sunset/lookout
12 back view looking at scenery

Recommended export:
- transparent WEBP
- approximately 600 × 600 px
- Oliver centered with breathing room around him
- consistent watercolor/illustrated style
- no text baked into the image

IMPORTANT
---------
The app has a graceful fallback landscape if a weekly header has not been uploaded yet.
Missing Oliver art simply leaves that illustration area empty.
This means the code can be uploaded and tested before all 44 artwork assets are complete.
